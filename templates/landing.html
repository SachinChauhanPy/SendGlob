Why SendGlob

You shouldn't need four tools to run one workshop

Most teams stitch together a bulk mailer, a certificate design tool, a CSV cleaner, and an automation layer to glue them. Four subscriptions, fragile hand-offs, and per-email fees that grow faster than your audience.

The old way

  • Mailchimp or SendGrid for email ($30 to $200/mo plus per-email fees)
  • Sertifier or Canva for certificate design ($29 to $99/mo)
  • Manual CSV cleanup between every tool
  • Zapier to glue it together (another subscription, more failure points)
  • Your sender reputation tied to a shared IP pool

The SendGlob way

  • One dashboard for campaigns, certificates, and signatures
  • Send from your own SMTP account, so your reputation stays yours

  • Async queue with automatic retry and provider-aware throttling
  • Credits only for generated documents. Email is free, always
Features

Everything the workflow needs, nothing it doesn't

Six capabilities that replace an entire stack of marketing tools.

Bulk email from your own account

Connect any SMTP provider once. SendGlob sends through your account, so deliverability and sender reputation stay in your hands. No shared IP pools, no warm-up dance.

Certificates from a CSV

Upload your design, mark where each field sits, and SendGlob centres every participant's name between your coordinates, in your fonts. Hundreds of personalised PDFs in one run.

Signatures that look hand-placed

Upload a signature photo once; the background is removed automatically. Then sign per recipient from a CSV column, so the right signatory lands on the right certificate.

AI-drafted copy in seconds

Describe your campaign in plain English and Gemini drafts the subject line, preheader, headline, body, and CTA. Tweak in the live preview and send. No prompt engineering needed.

Schedule once, or repeat

Set a campaign for later, or make it recur weekly or monthly. Campaigns lock shortly before a run to protect what goes out, and you get a reminder email before it fires.

Live tracking and retry

Watch send progress in real time with open tracking built in. Failed deliveries retry automatically, and throttling keeps you inside your provider's limits.

How it works

From signup to sent in under five minutes

01

Connect your SMTP account

Enter your SMTP host, port, and app password. It is encrypted the moment you save it, and you can test the connection with one click.

02

Prepare content and recipients

Pick a template or let AI draft one. For certificates, upload your design, mark the field coordinates, add a signature. Then drop in your recipient CSV.

03

Send now, or schedule it

Hit send, or set a schedule and let it recur. The queue handles throttling, retries, and document generation while progress updates live in the dashboard.
