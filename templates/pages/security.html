Security & Trust

What SendGlob actually does to protect your data

Every item on this page describes a control implemented in the product today. Nothing here is a roadmap claim. If a control is not listed, we have not built it yet, and we will not pretend otherwise.

Your mail stays yours

Email is sent from your own SMTP account using an app password you create and can revoke at any time. SendGlob is not a mail relay, does not use shared IP pools, and does not read your mailbox. Your sender reputation stays under your control.

Encryption at rest

Your email app password and uploaded signature images are protected with industry-standard encryption before they are stored. Encryption keys are kept separate from the data they protect, and nothing sensitive is ever stored in readable form.

Private files, private links

Signatures and generated documents are never exposed at public links. Every file is delivered through your signed-in session and checked against your account on each request, so only you can open what you create.

Deletion that actually deletes

When you delete something it stops being available immediately, and after a short recovery window it is permanently removed from our systems, with every removal logged. Deleted signature images are erased from storage right away.

Abuse protection

Sign-up, sign-in, password reset, and the contact form are protected against bots and automated abuse. Bulk sending is governed by our Acceptable Use and Anti-Spam Policy, and accounts that violate it are suspended.

Your recipients' data

The recipient CSV data you upload stays under your control: SendGlob processes it only to send campaigns and generate documents on your behalf, with the same retention and security controls. You are responsible for having consent to contact your recipients; the Privacy Policy describes the controller/processor split.

Sub-processors

SendGlob relies on a short list of infrastructure providers, listed here for transparency:

  • Razorpay: payment processing (card and billing details never touch SendGlob servers)
  • Google: optional sign-in with Google (OAuth)
  • AWS: application hosting
  • Cloudflare: bot protection on sign-in and contact forms
