About

Why SendGlob exists

SendGlob started with a simple frustration: running one workshop should not require four subscriptions. Teams stitch together a bulk email service, a certificate design tool, a CSV cleaner, and an automation glue layer, then spend their evenings debugging the seams between them.

SendGlob puts the whole workflow in one dashboard. You connect your own SMTP account (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, or any provider), upload a recipient CSV, pick or AI-draft an email template, and optionally generate personalised, signature-stamped certificate PDFs rendered from your own design. Sending is paced automatically with retries built in, and progress is tracked live.

What we believe

Your reputation is yours

Mail goes out through your own account, never a shared IP pool.

Your data is yours

Your credentials and files are encrypted, private to your account, and permanently removed when you delete them.

Pricing should be honest

Email delivery is free; credits are spent only when a document is generated.

The Security page documents each of these claims against what is actually built, and nothing on this site describes a feature that has not shipped.

Who is behind it

SendGlob is built and operated by an independent developer, run lean and self-funded. Decisions are made close to the product, support replies come from the person who wrote the code, and there is no pressure to paper over gaps with marketing. What is on this site is what is in the product.
