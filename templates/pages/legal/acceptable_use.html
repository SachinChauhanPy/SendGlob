SendGlob sends email from your own account, so your behaviour directly affects your own sender reputation and the deliverability of every SendGlob user. This policy is deliberately strict.

1. Consent is mandatory

You may only send to recipients who have given you consent to be contacted, or with whom you have an existing relationship that lawfully permits the message (for example, participants of your event receiving their certificates). Purchased, rented, scraped, or harvested lists are prohibited.

2. No unsolicited bulk email

Unsolicited bulk email (spam) is prohibited regardless of content. Every bulk message must be one the recipient would reasonably expect to receive from you.

3. Prohibited content

You must not send content that is illegal in your jurisdiction or your recipients'; deceptive or fraudulent, including phishing and impersonation; malware or links to malware; sexually explicit material; or content that harasses or incites violence. Sender identity and subject lines must not be misleading.

4. Respect provider and destination policies

You must comply with your email provider's sending limits and bulk-sender rules (for example Gmail program policies) and with the policies of recipient domains. SendGlob's throttling helps you stay within limits, but the responsibility is yours.

5. Honour opt-outs

If a recipient asks to stop receiving your messages, you must remove them from your lists before your next send.

6. Enforcement

We review abuse signals and reports. Violations can lead to warnings, suspension, or termination of your account through our operations console, depending on severity. Egregious abuse (phishing, malware, fraud) results in immediate termination and, where appropriate, reports to the relevant providers or authorities.

7. Reporting abuse

To report abuse involving a SendGlob user, contact {{ contact_email }} or use the contact form. Include the message headers where possible.
