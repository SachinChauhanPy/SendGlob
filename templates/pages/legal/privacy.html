1. Who is responsible

SendGlob is operated by {{ legal_name }} ("we"). For the personal data you provide about yourself, we are the controller. For personal data about your email recipients that you upload (see section 4), you are the controller and we process it only on your instructions.

2. Data we collect

Account data: email address, password (stored hashed), optional name, organization, phone, country, and timezone. Billing data: billing name and address, payment records and receipts (card details are handled by Razorpay and never stored by us). Configuration data: your SMTP settings, with the app password encrypted at rest. Content you upload: email templates, document designs, recipient CSV files, and signature images (signature files are encrypted at rest). Usage data: campaign and delivery statistics, including open tracking, and standard technical logs (IP address, user agent). Contact messages you send us, including IP address and user agent for abuse prevention.

3. Why we process it

To provide the Service (send campaigns, generate documents, track delivery); to process payments and maintain required financial records; to secure the Service (authentication, CAPTCHA, rate limiting, abuse prevention); to respond when you contact us; and to meet legal obligations. We process your data on the basis of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in securing and improving the Service, your consent where required, and legal compliance.

4. Your recipients' data

Recipient lists you upload (names, email addresses, and any custom CSV columns) are processed solely to send your campaigns and generate your documents. We do not use recipient data for our own purposes, do not enrich it, and do not contact your recipients except on your instruction. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to contact them. Deleting a campaign or your account removes this data through the deletion cycle in section 7.

5. Cookies

The Service uses only functional cookies: a session cookie to keep you signed in, a CSRF cookie to protect forms, and Cloudflare Turnstile cookies on protected forms. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

6. Security measures

SMTP app passwords and signature files are encrypted at rest using industry-standard authenticated encryption, with encryption keys held separately from the data they protect. Generated documents and signatures are accessible only through your authenticated account. Access to operator tooling is restricted and operator actions are logged. The Security page describes these controls in detail.

7. Retention and deletion

Deleted records become inaccessible immediately and are permanently removed after a retention window (90 days by default), with removals logged. Deleted signature images are removed from storage immediately. Financial records (payments, receipts, credit ledger) are retained as required for accounting and tax purposes.

8. Sub-processors

We share data only with the providers needed to run the Service: Razorpay (payments), Google (optional sign-in with Google), AWS (hosting), and Cloudflare (Turnstile CAPTCHA). We do not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers.

9. International transfers

The Service is operated from {{ jurisdiction }} and hosted on AWS. If you use the Service from another country, your data will be transferred to and processed in the hosting region. We apply the same protections regardless of location.

10. Your rights

You may access and correct your account data in the app, and request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data by contacting {{ contact_email }}. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law.

11. Children

The Service is not directed at children and may not be used by anyone under 18.

12. Changes

We will post updates to this policy on this page with a new effective date, and will highlight material changes.
