1. Credit packs

Credit packs are prepaid digital goods delivered instantly to your account. Once credits are delivered they are generally non-refundable. Unused credits do not expire while your account remains open.

2. Exceptions: when we do refund

We will refund a payment in full where the charge failed to deliver credits, where the same purchase was charged twice, or where a charge was unauthorized. Report the issue to {{ contact_email }} with the payment reference and we will investigate promptly.

3. Subscriptions

Subscription plans renew automatically each billing cycle. You can cancel at any time from the billing dashboard; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period and there is no pro-rata refund for the remainder of a period already charged. Credits already granted for the current period remain usable.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through Razorpay. Processing timelines depend on your bank or card issuer, typically 5 to 10 business days after the refund is initiated.

5. How to request a refund

Email {{ contact_email }} or use the contact form with your account email, the payment reference (from your receipt), and a short description of the issue. We aim to respond within 3 business days.

6. Account termination

If we terminate your account for a violation of the Acceptable Use and Anti-Spam Policy, remaining credits are forfeited. If you close your account in good standing, you may request a review of any unused paid credits at our reasonable discretion.
